About

I like the unglamorous work that keeps websites dependable.

I'm Febelyn, a front-end WordPress developer with 10+ years of experience building and maintaining websites for agencies and clients. A large part of my work starts with a finished design and ends with a responsive, editable WordPress site that keeps the details of that design intact.

That means debugging layout issues, tracing plugin conflicts, checking forms, testing responsive behavior, and making sure a small production update doesn't break something elsewhere.

I work remotely and I'm comfortable in written communication: task comments, Slack, documentation, and staging environments. I'm used to established agency workflows.

Based in UTC+08:00, available for long-term remote roles.
