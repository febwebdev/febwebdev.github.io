Questions recruiters ask me

Do you only do the front end?

Front-end is my strength, but I handle the whole WordPress side too: content structure with ACF and custom post types, forms and email delivery, WooCommerce, and keeping live sites running.

Which time zone are you in?

UTC+8. I've worked asynchronously with agencies in other time zones for over five years, using task comments, Slack and clear handoff notes.

Can you work with our page builder?

Most likely. I've worked in Elementor, Beaver Builder, Divi, Bricks, WPBakery, SiteOrigin and Avada, and I adapt to whatever a project already uses.

When can you start?

Right away. I usually reply within one business day.

What kind of role are you looking for?

A long-term, remote position on a team that builds or looks after WordPress sites. I'm also comfortable in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I take on select projects and ongoing site care.

Do you work on existing sites, or only new builds?

Both. A lot of my work is on live sites: layout fixes, plugin conflicts, form problems and content updates, alongside new builds from Figma.

Will our clients be able to edit the site themselves?

Yes, that's one of my main goals. I build with reusable blocks and patterns, and use ACF fields and custom post types so routine changes happen in WordPress, not in code.

How do you make sure nothing breaks?

I check every page at desktop, tablet and phone sizes, test in the main browsers (with BrowserStack or LambdaTest), and send test entries through every form before I call a job done. After a change, I look over the rest of the site too, so a small fix doesn't break something somewhere else.

How do you keep the team updated?

In writing, so nobody has to wait for a meeting: short updates in task comments, Slack, ClickUp or Asana, blockers flagged early, and notes the next person can follow.

Do you use AI tools?

I'm learning to bring them into my work. AI is changing how websites get built, and it can speed up the routine parts. My job is to make sure what it helps build is correct, works on every screen and in every browser, and is easy for clients to edit.

Why are client names missing from your work?

Most of my work was for agencies, so I leave client names out on purpose. I'm happy to walk you through any project in more detail.

Hiring, or need a hand with a site?

Send me a few lines about the role or the project. I'll reply within one business day.
