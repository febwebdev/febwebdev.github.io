About

Reliable implementation, thoughtful details, and clear written communication.

I'm Febe, a front-end WordPress developer with 8+ years of experience building and maintaining websites for agencies and clients. A large part of my work is translating finished designs into responsive, editable WordPress sites while preserving the details that make the design feel intentional.

I also enjoy the less glamorous work that keeps websites dependable: debugging layout issues, checking forms, testing responsive behavior, tracing plugin conflicts, and making sure small production updates don't create new problems elsewhere.

I work remotely and am comfortable collaborating through written project tools, task comments, Slack, documentation, and Git-based workflows.

How I work

  1. 01
    Understand the goal

    Review the design, requirements, existing stack, and editor workflow.

  2. 02
    Build for reuse

    Create responsive components and content structures that are easy to maintain.

  3. 03
    Test the details

    Check breakpoints, content edge cases, interactions, forms, and browser behavior.

  4. 04
    Communicate clearly

    Share concise updates, flag blockers early, and document anything the next person needs.
